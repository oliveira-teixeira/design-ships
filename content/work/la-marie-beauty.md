## Overview

La Marie Beauty is a med spa, and Beauty Experts is the platform it set out to build and license to other med spas. The client's notes set the scope early: communication between a spa and its clients, with no point of sale and no scheduling. Clients message the whole team or a chosen provider, see the treatments their provider logged on a calendar, and press and hold a treatment to open its aftercare instructions. The product has three roles, admin, MedSpa team and customer, across a web app and a native app. I worked in the design file from October 2024 to July 2025 with the other Fetchly designers on the account.

## Challenge

Med spa clients juggle injectables, facials and several skincare products, each with its own instructions. The spa team needed one inbox where unread messages move to the top when they wait too long, and the owner needed oversight of those conversations. Clients needed to see what their provider logged, the order to apply each product, and the rewards they earn in the programs they already use. The client described the look in their own words: European clean, neutrals with bright colors, masculine and feminine energy paired, with Glossier as the reference.

## Approach

For the MedSpa team, the web app opens on a dashboard with new clients, prescriptions, procedures done and rewards points, next to the message inbox. The client list uses cards with a context menu for quick actions. Each client page pairs the skincare products recommended to that client with a calendar of their treatments, and a procedures view lists every treatment by date.

For customers, the mobile app carries the same brand with tabs for skincare, calendar and profile. The calendar shows past appointments with search by procedure or name, and a chat with the provider is always one tap away, with read receipts on every message.

The file moved from wireframes for each role to an original design and a V2, and it ends with a breakpoints reference page so developers can see how each screen adapts from desktop to mobile.

## Outcome

The team delivered a complete design for three roles: a web app for admins and MedSpa teams and a native app for customers, taken from wireframes through an original design and a V2, with a breakpoints reference for development. The customer app covers messaging with read receipts, the treatment calendar and the skincare routine. The MedSpa side covers the dashboard, client records, product recommendations and procedures.
