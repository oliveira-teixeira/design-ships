## Overview

Estúdio Yawará presented I.A. Canvas Studio, a workshop on putting AI tools to work, scheduled for Sunday, March 23, 2025. I designed the identity for both names in Figma between late January and the end of March 2025: the Yawará wordmark and ibis logomark, the I.A. Canvas Studio lettering, and the Instagram stories, posts and banners that announced the event. I also built the workshop deck, with an agenda that moves from principles to tools to hands on practice. Branding has been part of my practice since my freelance years in Brasília, and this is the most complete recent example.

## Challenge

The identity had to hold two names without letting them compete. Yawará is the studio and I.A. Canvas Studio is the event, and each had to read clearly on a phone, in a story, a square post and a wide banner. For a workshop about AI, the brand leans on print traditions: a script wordmark and a bird with the weight of a woodcut, set in warm crimson. The palette also had to work both ways, light marks on crimson and dark marks on a pale ground.

## Approach

The wordmark is a flowing script with a slight bounce, used in cream on crimson and in crimson on sand. The logomark is an ibis with the weight of a woodcut print, used in cream on crimson and in black on a washed green.

The event name, I.A. Canvas Studio, is set in the same script as the wordmark, so the workshop reads as part of the studio. Instagram stories stack the event name over a small ibis, and a square post signed Estúdio Yawará apresenta ties the two names together.

A post for the design session uses an illustration of people around a table working through cards and sketches, in the same crimson and cream. Wide banners and square boards crop the ibis large, so its lines fill the frame behind the event name.

## Outcome

The identity covers the Yawará wordmark and ibis logomark in three color treatments, the I.A. Canvas Studio lettering, and a set of Instagram stories, square posts, wide banners and square boards. The Figma file also keeps the seven logo options I explored before settling on the final marks. The workshop deck I built for the event uses its own layout and walks through AI agents, a prompt style exercise and meeting assistants.
