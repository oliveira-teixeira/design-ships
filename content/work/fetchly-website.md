## Overview

Fetchly is a product team for hire: engineering, design, QA and a dedicated project manager on one flat monthly plan. The website has to make that offer clear to a founder who is comparing agencies. I directed the V2 program from March to August 2025, from competitor benchmarking and a brand kit through the sitemap, wireframes, page designs and the Webflow build. Since October 2025, as Director of Design, I own the site. I design the pages in Figma and build the harder sections myself as React code components for Webflow.

## Challenge

Several of Fetchly's key pages depended on paid traffic for their visitors. The offer had also changed. Clients now buy an embedded team on a monthly plan, and new service lines such as DesignOps, AI Forward Development and Growth needed pages of their own. Each page had to make a flat monthly price easy to understand for someone comparing agencies, and it had to earn search traffic on its own. Every new section also had to follow the brand book, with Marjorie and Inter type and the Malibu palette, and stay editable in Webflow by people who do not write code.

## Approach

**V2 program, 2025**

I directed competitor benchmarking, a brand kit, the sitemap, wireframes and page designs through to the Webflow build, and I owned the lead generation pages and the contact form.

**Service pages, 2026**

I designed and built the DesignOps, AI Forward Development, Growth and testimonial pages, each with its own proof and call to action.

**Code component library**

I wrote a library of Webflow code components in React and TypeScript, including the navbar, footer, plan estimator, testimonial carousels, case study slider and tech stack visuals. Each one is wired to the site's Webflow variables for color and type and exposes its content as props in the Designer. The source lives in one versioned repository.

**Performance**

I audited homepage delivery for PageSpeed, rebuilt the dashboard hero so it scales with CSS alone, and moved schema scripts out of the page head.

## Outcome

Organic search now brings more than 1,000 users a month to pages that relied on paid traffic. LinkedIn traffic to the site is up 163%, and tracked events are up nearly 400% year over year. Anyone editing the site can place the code components in the Webflow Designer and change them through props without touching code, and every component can be rebuilt and redeployed from the repository.
