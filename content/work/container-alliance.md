## Overview

Container Alliance is the largest shipping container provider network in the United States, renting and selling containers across North America. I joined the account in mid 2024 as the embedded designer from Fetchly's DesignOps service, working inside the client team, and I have stayed on it as the work moved from design into delivery. The engagement covers two connected products: the public website, where buyers find inventory and build quotes, and an internal platform that runs sales, wholesale orders and deliveries. Today I direct design on the account, including the delivery and driver logistics module.

## Challenge

Sales ran across three portals, Organizations, Wholesale and Stacks Admin, plus outside tools for ordering, quoting, packing slips, ERP, rental orders, fleet, drivers, storage locations, CRM and email, maps and accounting. Lead capture, quotes, invoices, payments and customer email lived in different places, so no one saw the full sales cycle in one view. The public site was slow and dated, and the 2024 review flagged navigation, search, pricing display and the conversion funnel. The two problems had to be solved together, because the site sends leads into the CRM and pulls live product and depot data back out of it.

## Approach

I ran the June to October 2024 roadmap for the site: navigation, search, pricing display, the conversion funnel, SEO, and a brand revamp with a new type hierarchy and palette.

In September 2024 I wrote the concept of operations that merged the three portals into one CRM. It defines leads, opportunities, quotes and the quote builder, customers, orders, products, solutions, depots, price books, units, tags and templates. I inventoried every legacy tool, mapped data across the old platforms for the migration, and delivered requirement specifications to the development team.

The system grew to more than 1,200 custom components across 6 product lines, from the Stacks CRM WorkHub and the wholesale quote screen to the mobile quote drawer. It connects to CallRail for calls, Zoho for invoicing, Stripe for payments, Hygraph for content, Postmark for email and Interchange for products.

The website relaunched in July 2025 with an SEO migration program and A/B testing.

## Outcome

By my platform inventory, one system now replaces 13 separate tools and runs the whole sales cycle, from lead capture and quotes to invoices, payments and customer email. The website relaunched in July 2025 with the SEO migration and A/B testing in place, and buyers get instant quotes from live product data in the CRM. The delivery and driver logistics module went live in July 2026, bringing fleet and drivers into the same platform. The engagement is still running, and I direct design on it.
