## Overview

Range Supply sells parts for sewer cleaning and hydro excavation trucks: nozzles, hoses, pumps and excavation equipment. Leadership sees ecommerce as the growth engine for the aftermarket parts business, so Fetchly ran the account as a pilot of a new model, with design, two developers and RevOps working in parallel. I directed the design workstream from the kickoff in February 2026 and designed the new home page.

## Challenge

Discovery found a store running an out of the box theme with almost no link to M2M, the ERP behind it. Most visitors arrived from search already holding a part number or an OEM number, and the home page gave them nowhere obvious to put it. Account customers wanted to reorder by part number and see their own pricing, neither of which the storefront understood.

## Approach

**Discovery**

Kickoff, discovery calls, and a CSD matrix of certainties, suppositions and doubts. We mapped the buyer experience as it stood and benchmarked home, listing and product pages against other parts sellers.

**Information architecture**

A new sitemap and navigation, informed by how buyers in this category actually hunt for a part.

**Home page**

A Shopify Horizon home page built around the way people arrive: search first, then the categories that carry the most demand, then the proof a buyer needs before trusting a supplier with a truck that is off the road. Reviewed as wireframes before it went to high fidelity.
